Verein für Musik- und Bewegungsperformance (Society for Music and Movement Performances)<br><br>
[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/allerdings.verein/) <br><br>
The aim of our charitable organisation is to support and develop artistic projects in the field of music
and movement performance. The three founding members are [Caterina Vögel](https://caterinavoegel.framer.website/), [Antonia Luksch](https://antonialuksch.framer.website/) and
Hannes Taljaard. We are all trained in the Viennese tradition of music and movement education (sometimes called 'Wiener Rhythmik') and
our subprojects explore the artistic potential of our training. The society was founded in June 2024. Highlights of the first two years of <i>allerdings</i> were a residency in Luxembourg (2025) to work on **Kleine Dinge**, and a visit to the University in Stellenbosch, South Africa (2026), to work on **Singing that moves / Singen das bewegt**.<br><br>
Our creative practice is in its essence co-creative: we work together as a team to develop concepts, strategies and pieces. We prefer to also work collaboratively with our ‘target audiences’ to develop performances that contain much more than our own ideas. Through our creative practice we hope to support people to imagine all the ways their worlds exist, and can exist, in order to deepen their experiences of being in the world, and thus to influence the meanings that they create in and through their lives. We hope to inspire others to contribute to a caring and just world that offers rich experiences

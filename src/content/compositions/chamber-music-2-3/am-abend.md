This work was also composed for the Trio Auturja, just like <i>Two songs for three Instruments (2025)</i>, and it is specifically dedicated to Junko Tsuchiya. This work also takes the lives of children and parents as inspiration. The first movement is a collage of two songs from my song cycle <i>Sing saam met My! (2014)</i> – specifically the songs <i>Wiegeliedjie</i> (Lullaby) and <i>Speelgoedliedjie</i> (Toy song). The second song is a version of <i>Liedjie-vir-as-baba-nie-kan-slaap-nie</i> which closes <i>Sing saam met My!</i>.

The two songs in this collection can be performed separately. If they are performed together, they should be performed in this order with a short silence between the two songs.
1. Kinderszenen (c.7’10“)
2. Liedchen (c.1‘00”)


Total duration c. 8’20”

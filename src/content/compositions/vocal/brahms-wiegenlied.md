vocal quartet (SATB) and piano

This composition is an arrangement, almost a recomposition, of the well-known lullaby by Johannes Brahms. The vocal lines and textures can be understood as reflections or even meditations on the complex inner world of adults who sing lullabies. The piano part is not an accompaniment of the usual kind – fragments from the piano music of Johannes Brahms are presented as an affective world in which the vocal experiences are embedded. As a whole, this complex composition is an attempt to make sense of the relationships between parents and children, and the intricate processes of enactment through which relationships grow. A short analysis of this composition is presented by Rudolf de Beer in his article An Overview of the choral music of Hannes Taljaard.

Duration c. 4 minutes

soprano, violin, cello, and piano; alternative versions exist for soprano and piano; and for baritone and piano; there is also a version for baritone, piano, cello and harp. Transposed versions for other voice types can be requested. 
The composition works well in dramatised versions, such as the one that I performed with friends in Vienna in November 2024. (Documented on the allerdings page on my website, and listed on the performance page.)
Just before bed time, the mother sings a song to encourage her child to put away all the toys. Then they sing another song together, to as for protection against all things unknown and scary. But the smaller one has not yet exhausted all play-energy and so a funny counting rhyme (about ducks) must be performed. The mother’s inventiveness surpasses her child’s curiosity and sleep creeps into the little one, a canvas on which she expresses her adoration for her beautiful child in the second last song. The last song transcends our small drama to suggest that we can create a world in which acceptance can be more powerful than expectations.
This composition is a collection, and the five works can be performed in any combinations and in different orders. I also wrote the texts for the songs. A document containing the Afrikaans texts of the songs can be downloaded here.

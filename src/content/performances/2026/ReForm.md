This performance will emerge through shared authorship and with Alexander Okhotnikov as project leader. The performers will contribute something from their own practice to the co-creative process, for example, sounds, movements, ideas, or ways of working. The performance will form part of a symposium on Arts, Climate and Research, organised by the Artistic Research Centre of the University of Music and performing Arts in Vienna (mdw). The performance and its preparation are part of the creative practice Pas: Trans_form, and contribute to reaching the aims of the project Zukunftspotenzial Kunst (Future Potential of Art). Read more [here](/assets/ReForm.pdf).

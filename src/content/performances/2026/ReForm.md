This performance emerged through shared authorship and with Alexander Okhotnikov as project leader. The performers contributed something from their own practice to the co-creative process, for example, sounds, movements, ideas, or ways of working. The performance formed part of a presentation by <a href="https://stephanieguse.com" target="_blank">Stephanie Guse</a> during the symposium on Arts, Climate and Research, organised by the Centre for Sustainability and the Artistic Research Centre of the University of Music and performing Arts in Vienna (mdw). The performance and its preparation are part of the creative practice <a href="https://hannestaljaard.com/projects/pastransform" target="_blank">Pas: Trans_form</a>, and contribute to reaching the aims of the project Zukunftspotenzial Kunst (Future Potential of Art). Read more [here](/assets/ReForm.pdf).

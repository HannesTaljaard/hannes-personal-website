<b>AMBO</b> performed <i>Arena Caiente</i>, a composition for two pianos by Alexander, and two pieces from a collection of piano duets, titled <i>NoWHere</i> which I am currently composing for us. From this collection, we gave the first perormances of <i>after Grief</i> and of <i>within Acceptance</i>. The main focus of the concert fell on our improvisations on the two pianos, and we also interacted with the audience via specific objects which became the basis for structuring an improvisation in sound. We ended our concert with an inside-piano sound improvisation, and were joined by dance students of the University of Cape Town. We worked with these wonderful young artists during our workshop <i>Improvisation</i> skills in Sound+Movement as a resource for dancers one week earlier, and their joining us on stage was a highlight of our tour in the Western Cape. Our concert was the conclusion of our workshops in piano improvisation at the South African College of Music (SACM).

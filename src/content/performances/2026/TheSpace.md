A composition by Hannes Taljaard for violin and viola titled <i>Amare Illi</i> formed the starting point for the development of this performance. The participants worked with the transformation of meaning through music, movement material and the Italian text of a composition by Giulio Caccini, <i>Amarilli mia bella</i>. This co-creative process was based upon the strategies for collaboration that are being developed via the creative practice <a href="https://hannestaljaard.com/projects/pastransform" target="_blank">Pas: Trans_form</a>. As part of this practice, the performance and its preparation supported and illuminated the aims of the project Zukunftspotenzial Kunst (Future Potential of Art). The performance formed part of a symposium on Arts, Climate and Research, organised by the University of Music and performing Arts in Vienna (mdw). Read more [here](/assets/TheSpace.pdf).

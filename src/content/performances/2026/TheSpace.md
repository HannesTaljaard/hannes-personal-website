A composition by Hannes Taljaard for violin and viola titled <i>Amare Illi</i> forms the starting point for the development of this performance. The participants will investigate the transformation of meaning by working with music, movement material and the Italian text of a composition by Giulio Caccini, <i>Amarilli mia bella</i>. This co-creative process is based upon the strategies for collaboration that are being developed via the creative practice Pas: Trans_form. As part of this practice, the performance and its preparation support and illuminate the aims of the project Zukunftspotenzial Kunst (Future Potential of Art). The performance will form part of a symposium on Arts, Climate and Research, which is currently organised by the Artistic Research Centre of the University of Music and performing Arts in Vienna (mdw).

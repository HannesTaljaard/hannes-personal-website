This performance took place as part of a concert "Preludium inför Nobel" with the subtitle Peace / Giving Space / Taking Space. The concert was inspired by the life of Desmond Tutu (Nobel Peace Prize) and musicians from KMH and from the North-West University (South Africa) performed.
